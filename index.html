OPERATING PROTOCOL

How we build high-performing websites

A structured process for custom web design, development, SEO, and launch-ready digital experiences.

01 BLUEPRINT

Rough Design & Blueprint

We start with structure, not pixels. Using your direction as the anchor and our own design instincts as the engine, we draft a wireframe — spatial decisions, content hierarchy, interaction logic — before any visual styling begins.

02 CRITIQUE

Multi-Agent Design Review

The draft goes through a structured critique process — reviewed across different design lenses and use-case scenarios. Weak points are identified and rebuilt before the client ever sees it. This is where most studios skip ahead; we don't.

03 APPROVAL

Client Demonstration

You see the improved version. We walk through every decision together — what's there, why it's there, and what we're ready to change. The final design is locked only once you've signed off. No surprises in the build.

04 BUILD

Programming

Development begins against the agreed spec — no theme kits, no page builders. Every component is written by hand to the exact interaction model we designed together. The approved blueprint is what ships to code, not what gets forgotten halfway through.

05 POLISH

Final Touches

We layer in the details that turn a working site into a finished one — yours and ours. Client-requested refinements come first. Then we add the micro-interactions, transitions, and edge-case handling we know make the difference between delivered and done.

06 AUDIT

Security, SEO & Optimisation

Full verification pass: security headers, SEO metadata, Core Web Vitals, accessibility, cross-browser rendering, and error checking. Nothing ships with a known gap. This isn't an afterthought — it's a gating step.

07 REFINE

Near-Perfect Push

We iterate against the audit findings until the product is as close to flawless as the timeline and scope allow. Scores, render quality, interaction feel — everything is benchmarked. Shipping mediocre work isn't an option; it's not what either of us should want on the record.

08 DEPLOY

GitHub Publication

The finished build goes live via GitHub — version-controlled, documented, and deployed to a stable public URL. Every commit is clean and readable. What goes to your hands is production-grade, not a dev build with promises attached.

09 HANDOVER

Payment & Full Ownership Transfer

Payment confirmed — then every asset, repository, and access credential transfers to you completely. Full ownership. No licensing, no lock-in, no ongoing dependency on us unless you choose it. The work is yours, not ours to hold over you.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions

Everything businesses usually ask us before starting a project. Can't find your answer here? Reach out directly.

01 How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites take between 3 and 6 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. A simple one-page site can be ready in under two weeks, while larger multi-page or e-commerce builds can take 6-8 weeks. We'll give you a firm timeline after our first briefing call.

02 How much does a website cost?

Pricing starts from ₹22,000 for website development and varies based on pages, features, SEO scope, and design complexity. Check our Pricing section for a full breakdown, or reach out for a custom quote tailored to your project.

03 What's included in the price?

Every project includes design, development, mobile responsiveness, basic SEO setup, and a full personal review before launch. Hosting, domain registration, and ongoing maintenance are available as add-ons if you'd like us to manage them for you.

04 Do I own the website once it's built?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, you own the website, its code, and all its content outright. We don't lock you into proprietary platforms or hold your site hostage.

05 Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive, meaning it adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops automatically. We test across real devices before handing anything off.

06 Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance plans covering updates, security monitoring, backups, and small content changes. You can also choose to manage the site yourself — we'll hand over everything you need.

07 Can I update the website content myself?

Yes, if you'd like a content management system (CMS), we can build your site so you can edit text, images, and blog posts without touching any code. Just let us know during planning.

08 Do you review the website before it's handed over?

Yes — I personally review every website myself, end to end, until it's fully polished. Layout, content, responsiveness, performance — I go through it all before you ever see a rough draft. There's no fixed cap on 'revision rounds'; I keep refining on my own until it's right, then walk you through the finished result.

09 Do you design the website or just build it?

Both. We handle everything from visual design and brand alignment to development, testing, and launch. You won't need to hire a separate designer and developer.

10 What if I already have a logo and brand guidelines?

Even better. We'll build your website around your existing brand identity — colors, fonts, and tone — to keep everything consistent across your business.

11 What if I don't have a logo or brand yet?

That's fine too. We can help you shape a simple visual identity as part of the project, or point you toward the right direction before we start building.

12 Will my website show up on Google?

We build every site with SEO fundamentals in place — clean code, fast load times, proper headings, metadata, and technical structure. Ranking well over time also depends on content, backlinks, and consistency, which we can advise on separately.

13 Do you build online stores / e-commerce sites?

Yes, we build e-commerce fronts with product listings, cart, and checkout functionality. Let us know your expected catalog size and payment gateway preferences so we can scope it properly.

14 Can you redesign my existing website instead of building from scratch?

Yes. We regularly redesign outdated sites — improving speed, structure, SEO performance, and visual design while keeping the content and search history that already works in your favor.

15 Who pays for hosting and domain?

You handle your own hosting and domain purchase — that keeps the account fully in your name and keeps your costs down. I'll guide you through picking and connecting everything during the build, but I don't take on ongoing hosting or domain management as a service — my time stays focused on design and development work.

16 How do payments work?

We typically work with a 50% deposit to begin the project and the remaining balance due at launch. For larger projects, we can split payments into milestones — this gets confirmed in your project agreement.

17 What if I need to cancel the project midway?

We understand plans change. If a project is cancelled after work has started, you're billed only for the work completed up to that point, based on the agreed milestones.

18 How do we communicate during the project?

We keep it simple — email and WhatsApp/calls for quick updates, with milestone check-ins so you always know where things stand. No project management software to learn.

19 What industries do you typically work with?

Our live portfolio spans local retail and e-commerce — a pharmacy, a neighbourhood general store, and a fashion boutique — each built as a full ordering experience, not just a brochure site. If your business sells products or services and needs a website that actually converts visitors into customers, we're a strong fit.

20 What happens after my website goes live?

We do a final walkthrough, hand over any logins and files you need, and stay available for support. If you're on a maintenance plan, we continue monitoring and updating the site for you.
